PRINT PROJECT / 01

Vision Magazine

Developed a seven-page magazine series over 12 weeks, taking each page through an iterative process of design, critique, and refinement. Using InDesign, Photoshop, and Illustrator, I created layouts and visual elements for covers, editorial content, and a feature spread, while also extending the printed piece with an interactive AR experience. The project concluded with a production day where the final pages were printed and bound into a collective class publication.

PRINT PROJECT / 02

Playing Cards

I created a Mexican inspired playing card deck and made it a print ready physical product. Using Illustrator and InDesign, I developed the card designs, managed the multi-page layout, prepared the deck for imposition, and created my first packaging dieline for the finished cards.

PRINT PROJECT / 03

Every Door Direct Mail Marketing Postcard

I developed an EDDM postcard marketing campaign from initial concept through final production. Working closely with the Creative Director, I created moodboards and preliminary concepts, incorporated feedback through multiple rounds of iteration, and refined the selected direction into a final design that was produced and printed.
